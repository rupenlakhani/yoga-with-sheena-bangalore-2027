The Location

Bengaluru, India’s Garden City

Known as India’s Garden City, Bengaluru pairs a gentle, green climate with deep yogic and Ayurvedic roots. Beyond the city, the countryside opens into farmland, temples and quiet villages — a soulful, unhurried backdrop for turning inward.

October brings Bengaluru’s most beautiful weather: warm, sun-filled days around 27°C, cool evenings near 18°C, and the lush greenery of the tail end of the monsoon.

The Venue

Shreyas Retreat

We’ll be staying at Shreyas Retreat, an AYUSH-certified wellness retreat set on Santoshima Farm in Nelamangala, just outside Bengaluru. It blends the simplicity of ashram living with the comforts of a luxury resort — landscaped gardens, organic vegetable plots and a genuinely peaceful, healing atmosphere.

Guests have full access to the swimming pool and jacuzzi, the yoga pavilion and amphitheatre, a library and home theatre, a fully-equipped gymnasium, and the sauna and steam room.

Good to Know

FAQs

What should I bring?
A full packing list will be sent before the retreat; you’ll need your own yoga mat.
Is this suitable for beginners?
Yes — classes are designed for all levels. Please let Sheena know of any medical conditions beforehand.
Can you accommodate dietary needs?
All meals are vegetarian, with vegan options available. Allergies and specific needs can be accommodated with prior notice.
What is the cancellation policy?
Full terms and conditions will be shared at the time of booking.
I’m nervous about travelling alone — is that okay?
Completely normal! We’ll set up a WhatsApp group before the retreat so you can connect with fellow guests — our retreats are a warm, welcoming, like-minded community.
What fruits will be in season while we’re there?
October sits at the start of Bangalore’s citrus season, so you can expect fresh oranges alongside bananas, which are available year-round. It’s also the season for pomegranate, guava, papaya, sapota (chikoo) and custard apple (sitaphal) — expect plenty of these on the breakfast table and in fresh juices.
Which airlines fly into the nearest airport?
You’ll fly into Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), Bengaluru. British Airways and Virgin Atlantic each fly direct from London Heathrow, and Air India also operates a direct Heathrow–Bengaluru service — between them there are direct flights most days of the week. One-stop options (via hubs such as Dubai, Doha or Abu Dhabi with Emirates, Qatar Airways or Etihad) are also available if you’d prefer a wider choice of times and fares.
